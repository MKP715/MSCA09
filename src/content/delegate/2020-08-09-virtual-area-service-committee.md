**Pandemic Panel 70, COVID 19 Conference**

- On 7/22/2020 the Board voted unanimously for MSCA to contribute without request for reimbursement the $3,200 contribution Area 09 made in February 2020 before the Conference was cancelled. There is a requirement of $1,800 to support delegate’s travel, we contributed $3,200, and although we do not know what the per-delegate cost was for the virtual Conference was, it seemed appropriate to honor that contribution and not request reimbursement.
- Since 6/20/2020, I’ve provided report-backs to the following nine Districts: 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 17, 23, 24, 25 (the last three Hispanic Districts were in person). Over the next month, I have the following three report-backs scheduled: Districts 4 (9/2), 7 (8/10), and 12 (9/13), which will be 21 of 24 Districts. At least two Hispanic Districts have asked me to provide report-backs at the Group-level, although one of them cancelled due to a COVID-positive member.If not already, please schedule my report-back with the remaining Districts.
