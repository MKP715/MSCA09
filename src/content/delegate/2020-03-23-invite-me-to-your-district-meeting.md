Dear MSCA 09 “General Servants,”

If you are like me, one of the sincere pleasures of General Service is spending time with each of you on a monthly basis and with the Board twice a month. I’m finding that same pleasure participating in “virtual meetings,” both on Zoom and Blue Jeans, where there are AA meetings 24 hours a day, seven days a week (see http://www.247aaonline.com/).

I assume that many of you are meeting “virtually” at the District-level on a regular basis. If this is the case and you would like for me (and possibly other Board members) to attend your virtual meetings, this will be an excellent opportunity for you to ask questions of what is happening in our Area and in New York. This includes the status of the Conference and any questions you have about Agenda Items and how to register your group or District conscience. As your Panel 70 delegate, I can only assume that I am getting information straight from New York and I will do my best to keep you informed, face-to-face, virtually.

So, please send me the date, time, and connection information for your virtual meetings, and I will do my best to join your District meetings. I will maintain a calendar of available dates and get right back to you when I can and cannot attend your meetings.

 In Love and service, Ed.
